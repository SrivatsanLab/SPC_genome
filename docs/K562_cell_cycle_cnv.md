# K562 sc_PolE: cell cycle from coverage, replication-corrected copy number, and trees

Date: 2026-10-06. Follows `docs/K562_aneufinder_gc_review.md`. All runs use the 1000 sc_PolE_novaseq
cells (BAMs symlinked in `results/K562_tree/aneufinder_gc/input/`, aligned to UCSC hg38).
`results/` and `data/` are gitignored; every output below is regenerated by the scripts named.

## Why

On every copy-number run (1 Mb, 500 kb, 250 kb, repeat-masked, smoothed, bin- and fragment-level GC
corrected) the CNV dendrogram grouped cells mainly by a per-cell "GC bias" score and by noise
(adjacent cells in the same GC-bias quintile ~3x chance), not by population. That score turned out
to be replication: S-phase cells have early-replicating (GC-rich) DNA doubled. Population signal
on those trees was weak (~1.1x).

### Coverage (steps 4-6)

- Fragments: properly paired, both mates MAPQ >= 10, fragment = leftmost to rightmost mapped base
  (one count per fragment, unsequenced insert included), <= 1 kb, outside the ENCODE blacklist,
  duplicates (same chrom/start/end) removed. Example cell: 12.3 M pairs -> 1.22 M unique fragments
  (heavy WGA duplication). Median 727 k unique fragments per cell.
- Fragment-level GC: per cell, fragment GC (BSgenome UCSC hg38) vs an expected distribution of
  fragments built from uniquely mappable, unmasked, copy-number-weighted start positions with the
  cell's own fragment lengths; weight = expected / observed per GC percent (smoothed, clamped
  1/8-8). GC-rich fragments under-represented (weight ~2 at 60% GC, ~4.6 at 75%).
- Bins (250 kb): dropped if >= 50% repeat mask (centromeres, gaps, Satellite, segmental dups) or
  >= 50% N gap + blacklist, or < 90% uniquely mappable (Umap k100); 10,778 of 12,342 kept; depth
  divided by mappable fraction.
- Smoothing: each kept bin = mean of the two overlapping 500 kb windows (250 kb step),
  0.5 * left + self + 0.5 * right over kept adjacent neighbours.
- Segmentation: AneuFinder edivisive (`findCNVs`, R = 10, sig.lvl = 0.1): per-chromosome change
  points, counts / cell mean, copy number = round(segment mean x multiplier), multiplier 1.5-6
  chosen to put the profile closest to integers. No smoothing between bins otherwise.
- Fragment-level GC correction removes fragment-GC bias but leaves bin-scale GC-correlated
  variation (per-cell |rho(depth vs consensus, bin GC)| median 0.24 vs 0.03 for AneuFinder's
  bin-level correction): that residual is mostly replication (below).

### Cell-cycle phase from coverage (step 7)

- Coverage per bin: fragment-GC depth / mappable / consensus copy number (median integer CN of
  on-peak cells, GC-corrected 1 Mb run) / cell median, log2.
- Each chromosome (chr1-22, X) is an independent reporter: per chromosome, slope of log2 coverage
  on RT and a step fit (replicated above an RT threshold).
- Pass 1 (vs copy number): a tight peak of ~450 non-replicating cells at slope -0.55 (shared
  amplification bias against early, GC-rich, open regions) and an S-phase continuum to ~+1.0.
- Pass 2: the core of that peak (median slope < -0.4, <= 3 chromosomes positive; 388 cells) is the
  non-replicating reference; every cell re-measured against the reference median profile.
- S phase: sign test across chromosomes p < 0.01 and median slope > the reference cells' 99th
  percentile (0.158). Result: 594 S, 406 G1/G2. G1 and G2 cannot be separated (per-cell
  normalisation removes total DNA content); G0 likewise.
- Replicated fraction of S cells spans ~0.1-0.85, roughly uniform (asynchronous culture). S
  fraction ~60% is high versus typical K562 flow (35-45%); worth checking against EDF 17C flow.
- The earlier per-cell GC-bias score is largely this: rho -0.86 with the median replication slope.
- S fraction does not differ by population (chi-square p = 0.24).

### Replication regression and corrected copy number (step 8)

- Per cell, y = coverage vs the non-replicating reference; model y = c + log2(1 + a p(RT)),
  p = logistic(k (RT - t)); fit on medians of y in 50 RT classes (robust to CNVs); grid over t, k, a.
  S cells: a median 0.86 (fits r2 0.93-0.995); G1/G2: a median 0.04.
- Corrected depth = depth / mappable / 2^reference / (1 + a p(RT)) (the cell's own CNVs kept), then
  the same smoothing and edivisive segmentation.
- Effect: median per-chromosome RT slope S 1.02 -> 0.05, G1/G2 0.06 -> 0.01; segments per cell
  S 295 -> 174, G1/G2 188 -> 102; calls > 10 copies 0.20% -> 0.13% of cell-bins.
- Cells needing substantial correction: 594 S-phase (565 of the 956 non-artefact cells);
  643 with a >= 0.3; 124 G1/G2 cells with small a (0.1-0.3, likely at the edges of S).

### 1 Mb version (step 8b)

`scripts/utils/rtcorr_segment_1mb.R` -> `results/K562_tree/aneufinder_fragment_gc_rtcorr_1mb/output/`.
Each AneuFinder 1 Mb bin = mean replication-corrected depth of its kept 250 kb sub-bins (masking
applied at 250 kb; >= 2 of 4 sub-bins required): 2,731 bins. No smoothing; edivisive as in the
original 1 Mb run. Residual RT slope S 0.05, G1/G2 0.02; segments per cell S 59, G1/G2 57 (original
uncorrected 1 Mb run: 107 for both); median ploidy 2.97 (masked CN0 regions no longer pull it down);
815 of 1000 cells in [2.5, 3.0). Clean heatmap without artefact and noisy cells:
`genomeHeatmap_no_artefacts_rtcorr_1mb_quality.pdf` (882 cells).

### Baseline anchoring (step 8c)

AneuFinder's edivisive scales each cell by the multiplier (1.5-6) that puts its segments nearest
whole numbers; a profile and the same profile x 4/3 or x 2 fit about equally well, so some cells get
the dominant K562 state (3) called 2, 4, 5 or 6 with every state shifted. On the corrected 1 Mb
calls, among 882 good cells: 13 at 6 (clean x2 rescale, shape r 0.93 with consensus), 8 at 4, 3 at
5, 29 at 2 (mostly real-looking off-peak cells). `scripts/utils/anchor_baseline.R` fixes the scale
from the karyotype: baseline = the cell's median depth in bins where the consensus is 3; each
AneuFinder segment's copy number = round(3 x segment mean depth / baseline) (boundaries unchanged).
51 of 53 re-anchored to 3 (2 stay at 4); cells already at 3 change in 0.2% of bins; good cells at
the dominant ploidy 816 -> 852. Output `results/K562_tree/aneufinder_fragment_gc_rtcorr_1mb_anchored/`;
heatmap `genomeHeatmap_no_artefacts_rtcorr_1mb_anchored_quality.pdf`. Coverage cannot detect true
whole-genome changes, so anchoring removes only an unidentifiable scale.

## Trees and population tests

Distance tree: `K562_cnv_distance_tree.R` - copy number rescaled to the median ploidy and
rounded, distance = mean |CN difference| per bin, neighbour joining rooted on the K562 consensus.
Options: `K562_CELLS` = all / G1G2 / G1G2strict (G1/G2 with fitted a < 0.1);
`K562_MIN_EVENT_MB` resets deviations shorter than that to the consensus. Sister test:
`K562_cnv_tree_sisters.R` (cherries; nulls: labels, within cell-cycle stage, within spikiness
quintile, whole round-1 wells; same- vs different-well split). Barcode segment 4 is the round-1
well and encodes population by design.

Same-population sister pairs (expected ~18.4%):

| Tree | Pairs | Same pop. | Enrichment (p) | Wells shuffled |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| All 956 cells | 285 | 23.2% | 1.26x (0.026) | 1.21x (0.042) |
| All, events >= 3 Mb | 256 | 23.4% | 1.27x (0.026) | 1.27x (0.025) |
| All, events >= 5 Mb | 231 | 22.5% | 1.22x (0.068) | 1.18x (0.083) |
| G1/G2 (391) | 110 | 26.4% | 1.44x (0.027) | 1.55x (0.003) |
| G1/G2, >= 3 Mb | 91 | 34.1% | 1.86x (0.0003) | 1.44x (0.005) |
| G1/G2, >= 5 Mb | 86 | 30.2% | 1.64x (0.005) | 1.43x (0.018) |
| G1/G2 strict (276), >= 3 Mb | 60 | 21.7% | 1.17x (0.32) | 1.07x (0.42) |

- G1/G2 >= 3 Mb: enrichment holds within noise quintiles (1.76x) and for both same-well (2.0x) and
  different-well (1.8x) sisters; P_0 strongest (3.0x, p = 0.005). Not robust to the strict set
  (fewer pairs, and a lower point estimate).
- All-cell trees still pair cells by cell-cycle stage (~1.7-2.2x) and noise even after
  replication regression; restricting to G1/G2 is what gives the clean population signal.
- Earlier dendrogram (clusterHMMs) neighbour test, `K562_cnv_tree_neighbours.R`: population
  adjacency 1.03-1.17x on all trees, tree dominated by GC bias / noise (2.3-3x).

CNV vs SBS tree (`sc_og_test.newick`), using `K562_tree_{topology,distance,sister_pairs,clade_membership}_concordance` / `_pairs` / `_membership`
scripts with `K562_CNV_TREE` and `K562_CONCORDANCE_TAG`: no cell-level agreement on the G1/G2 >= 3 Mb
(391 cells) or strict (276) sets - 0 shared cherries, clade matching and ARI at null, Mantel r
0.04 / -0.003. Each tree groups by population on its own (SBS sisters same population 26-31%,
p <= 0.011). The user does not regard the SBS tree as reliable lineage evidence; these
comparisons are descriptive.

SBS tree vs cell cycle (`K562_sbs_tree_cell_cycle.R`, 1000 cells): weak structure by replication
state - sisters share cell-cycle stage 1.18x (p = 0.014), patristic distance vs |delta DNA content|
rho = 0.088 (p = 0.001), S cells slightly further from the root (p = 0.0007; root-to-tip rho 0.27
with spikiness); de novo SBS counts and observed sites per cell do not differ by phase. Population
is the stronger signal (sisters 1.51x, p = 0.0002).

## Outlying and noisy cells

- The cells clustered at the edges of the corrected heatmap (100 furthest from the consensus) are
  mostly the off-peak cells from `K562_offpeak_ploidy.R`: 58 real-looking (large integer-level
  changes) and 28 artefacts; 14 on-peak. Not a cell-cycle correction failure: no residual RT slope,
  not enriched for S phase, same fitted amplitude; their lower replication-fit r2 follows from
  large copy-number changes dominating coverage. Distance tracks ploidy offset (rho 0.63) and
  spikiness (0.51), not depth.
- Artefact and noisy on-peak cells: ~20% fewer unique fragments, normal duplication rate, more
  uneven amplification (within-segment CV 0.31 vs 0.26; counting noise alone 0.14 vs 0.12; 3.6%
  dropout bins), so most spikiness is amplification unevenness, not depth. Patchy multi-bin
  deviations (spread further by the overlapping-window smoothing) are called as segments by
  edivisive, whose test assumes independent bins: 229 vs 144 segments. No doublet signature (fewer
  fragments, same de novo SBS per observed site). Real-looking outliers have more fragments (+34%),
  clean integer states and normal noise; a private-mutation VAF test would settle doublets.
- `K562_cell_noise.R`: within-segment coverage CV per cell against the cell's own segments; the
  distribution breaks at ~0.30. 91 cells flagged noisy (CV > 0.30, `K562_NOISE_MAX`). Noisy cells
  are unevenly spread across populations (P_3 14%, P_4 14%, P_0 4%, P_2 4%), so population tests
  should be checked without them. `K562_aneufinder_clean_heatmap.R` with `K562_DROP_NOISY=1` drops
  them: `genomeHeatmap_no_artefacts_rtcorr_250kb_quality.pdf` (882 cells).
